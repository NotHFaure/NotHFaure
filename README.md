## What's here

**[online-footprint-auditor](https://github.com/NotHFaure/online-footprint-auditor)** — a
local OSINT tool for auditing your own public footprint, then telling you what to do about
it. Python, with a self-hosted SearXNG backend for real
automated search rather than manual copy and paste.

**[Adversarial Attacks on Battery Energy Storage Systems](https://github.com/NotHFaure/Adversarial-Attacks-on-Battery-Energy-Storage-Systems-Using-Physics-Based-Models-in-PyBaMM)**
— co-authored research code. Adversarial perturbations injected into a physics-based
lithium-ion battery model in PyBaMM, to see whether attacks on battery telemetry are
detectable in terminal voltage, state of charge and state of health. Five authors: Alaa
Selim, Harrison Faure, Huadong Mo, Hemanshu Pota and Daoyi Dong.

**[Text-Based Predictive Models](https://github.com/NotHFaure/Text-Based-Predictive-Models-LSTM-GRU-RNN)**
— a word-level next-word predictor framed as an accessibility aid for people with ALS,
comparing LSTM, GRU and RNN architectures on the same corpus.
